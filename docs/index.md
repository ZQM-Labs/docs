## The current game: Wisp's Lost Lanterns

Wisp — a small, wandering will-o'-the-wisp — has lost her six glow lanterns, and teams follow the trail to relight them. *Wisp's Lost Lanterns* is the current ZQM Labs Halloween edition, running **October 30 – November 1, 2026**:

- 6 quest stops, each with a QR sign, a location story, and a puzzle
- 4 Dial levels — **Cozy** (family, all ages), **Spooky** (teens+), **After Dark** (adults), **Overdrive** (competitive offices) — the same stops play differently per level
- A hint ladder at every stop (3 hints; the third is the "Ranger assist")
- Offline-safe player pages that run in the phone browser — no app to install
- A free 6-stop sample quest, *Wisp's Warm-Up*, you can read or run in any neighborhood in about 30 minutes — no purchase, no signup

Meet the residents before you play: [Z's Zoo](https://zoo.zqmlabs.com) is the field guide to the creatures of the quest.

## Free sample quest

The sample quest is a complete mini edition of the game, published in the open [Quest Packs](https://packs.zqmlabs.com) library. Read it and solve all six stops at the table, or run it for real: pick 6 waypoints, generate printable QR signs with the [Organizer Toolkit](https://organize.zqmlabs.com), set the Dial, and play.
