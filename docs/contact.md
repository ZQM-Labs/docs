# Contact

**Email: [zqmcomputing@gmail.com](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com)**

That one inbox covers hosting questions, booking confirmations, day-of event support (monitored during every event window), feedback, and Communities directory listings.

## Asking about an event

To ask about a *Wisp's Lost Lanterns* event, include:

- Your organization or group
- Preferred dates
- Location
- The tier you're considering

The Lab replies — and **wait for confirmation before advertising an event as booked**. Availability depends on the current game, event window, venue, organizer setup, and support capacity.

## During your event

If something breaks mid-game, check [Day-Of Troubleshooting](hosting/troubleshooting.md) first — most issues are a lens wipe or a sign angle away from fixed. Still stuck? Email; the inbox is monitored throughout your event window.
