# Choosing a Tier

Four packages, all flat fees, all for the current game — *Wisp's Lost Lanterns*, the Halloween edition running October 30 – November 1, 2026. These are not universal prices for every ZQM Labs game; future titles may have different formats, access models, and prices.

## The tiers

### Family Night — $49 flat

For one household playing together. The kit is a six-stop quest with printable signs, puzzles, and hints.

**Halloween launch offer:** use code **FAMILY25** at checkout for $24 off — play for **$25**. The code applies to Family Night only.

### Community — $199 flat

For a neighborhood, HOA, PTA, church, or other community group — priced for unlimited families. Fundraising arrangements may be available for qualifying events; confirm the current terms with the Lab before promoting a fundraiser.

### Office Team — $349 flat

A team-building format for coworkers. Confirm the current scope, group size, and mechanics with the Lab before booking.

### Office Event — $499 flat

For a larger company or organization-wide event. Confirm the current scope, group size, mechanics, and availability with the Lab before booking.

## Youth groups and form-your-own

Youth groups and custom groupings are available by arrangement — ask the Lab about current availability, suitability, supervision, and pricing. These options are not confirmed bookings until the details are agreed.

## Buying

Checkout for all four tiers lives on the [hosting tiers page](https://zqmlabs.com/tiers/). One flat fee per kit — and the kit is yours to replay for your group.

Not sure which tier fits? Email [zqmcomputing@gmail.com](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com) with your group, preferred dates, and location, and the Lab will point you at the right one. Before announcing your event, confirm the details listed in [Hosting a Game](index.md#before-you-announce-your-event).
