# Choosing a Tier

Four packages, all flat fees, all for the current game — *Wisp's Lost Lanterns*, the Halloween edition running October 30 – November 1, 2026. These are not universal prices for every ZQM Labs game; future titles may have different formats, access models, and prices.

## The tiers

### Family Night — $49 flat

For one household playing together. The kit is a six-stop quest with printable signs, puzzles, and hints.

**Halloween launch offer:** use code **FAMILY25** at checkout for $24 off — play for **$25**. The code applies to Family Night only.

### Community — $199 flat

For a neighborhood, HOA, PTA, church, or other community group — priced for unlimited families. Fundraising arrangements may be available for qualifying events; coordinate the current terms with the Lab before promoting a fundraiser.

### Office Team — $349 flat

A team-building format for coworkers. Coordinate the current scope, group size, and mechanics with the Lab before announcing your event.

### Office Event — $499 flat

For a larger company or organization-wide event. Coordinate the current scope, group size, mechanics, and availability with the Lab before announcing your event.

## Youth groups and form-your-own

Youth groups and form-your-own groupings are available by arrangement — ask the Lab about current availability, suitability, supervision, and pricing. These options are not confirmed bookings until the details are agreed. Flat kits are generic and are not customized per buyer.

## Buying

Checkout for all four tiers lives on the [hosting tiers page](https://zqmlabs.com/tiers/). Your kit is usable on checkout. One flat fee per kit — and the kit is yours to replay for your group during the event window.

- **No refunds or reschedules.** All kit sales are final.
- **Kit access expires after the window.** Kit access expires after the October 30 – November 1, 2026 window.

Not sure which tier fits? Email [zqmcomputing@gmail.com](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com) with your group, preferred dates, and location, and the Lab will point you at the right one. Before announcing your event, coordinate the details listed in [Hosting a Game](index.md#before-you-announce-your-event).
