# After the Event

## The finale, finished

You already gathered at the last stop, tallied the scores, broke any ties, and handed out the finisher badges ([Running the Event](running-the-event.md#the-finale)). One thing left at the venue: **the group photo.**

## Photos and testimonials

Your photos and scores belong to you. They are shared with the Lab only if you choose to send them — and the Lab only uses them, for the community showcase and testimonials, **with permission, always**. If you'd like to share, email your favorites to [zqmcomputing@gmail.com](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com) after the event.

## Feedback

Tell the Lab what worked and what didn't — a sign nobody could scan, a puzzle everybody loved, a stop that needs a rewrite. Feedback goes to [zqmcomputing@gmail.com](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com), and the [Playtests](https://playtests.zqmlabs.com) site documents how playtest feedback shapes the game.

## Replay it

Your kit is one flat fee, **yours to replay for your group** — same signs, same route, new victims. Reprinting a weather-beaten sign? The [print specs](before-the-event.md#print-specs-qr-signs-that-scan) and the toolkit's QR generator have you covered.

## Join the Communities directory

Communities that run ZQM Labs games can be listed in the **Communities directory**, so players and prospective organizers near you can see that quests happen in your town.

- **Who qualifies:** a community is a host organization *or* an informal group — a library, community center, school, scout troop, HOA, parks department, local business, neighborhood crew, or club — that runs the games for its people. Formal and informal groups clear the same bar.
- **Verified and opt-in only:** every public listing is human-verified, and no license holder is ever auto-listed — you appear only if you opt in.
- **Booking-linked:** each listing carries a **"run this in your town"** checkout path, so a visitor who finds your community can start hosting one of their own.
- **Privacy:** listings show organizations and venues — never a home address, and never anything about who played. An individual family's Family Night is not listed.

To be listed, email [zqmcomputing@gmail.com](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com) after your event.
