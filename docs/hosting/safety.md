# Safety & Supervision

The short version: the host owns the venue and the route, the game owns the puzzles. These are the safety basics as documented across the ZQM Labs host materials.

## Route and signs

- **Never place signs where players must cross traffic to reach them** — wet or dry.
- Choose waypoints 2–4 minutes apart on foot, and note anything that could move or vanish (parked cars, decorations) when you pick them.
- Signs go at eye level with the QR facing the approach path, so players stop *at* the sign instead of hunting around it.

## Match the Dial to your group

The Dial is your age-and-intensity control. Set it for the group you actually have:

| Dial | Audience |
|---|---|
| **Cozy** | All ages |
| **Spooky** | Teens and up |
| **After Dark** | Adults |
| **Overdrive** | Competitive groups |

## Weather and the rain plan

- **Light rain:** play on — laminated signs survive it.
- **Heavy rain or storms:** postpone to the rain date you picked when you set up. Check the forecast the day before and decide by morning.
- When in doubt, postpone.

## Your responsibilities as host

For an in-person event, the organizer confirms a suitable venue and route for their participants — considering **accessibility, supervision, local permissions, weather, and safety** — and coordinates responsibilities with the Lab before announcing the event. That includes:

- Supervision arrangements appropriate to your group (youth groups: agree supervision with the Lab when you book).
- Any permission your venue requires — a park, school, HOA common area, or business property may have its own rules.
- A route your players can actually walk: teams move on foot, one phone per team, and stops can be as close as 20 feet apart if your space is small.

## Photos and scores

Your players' photos and scores are **yours** — they are only ever shared with the Lab for the community showcase with permission, always. See [After the Event](after-the-event.md).
