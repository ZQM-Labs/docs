# Hosting a Game

Every quest needs one host — the **Lab Assistant** — to print a few pages and place six signs. That is the whole job: about **one hour, total**. The Lab handles the game itself and supports you remotely on a limited, best-effort basis during your event window.

This handbook covers hosting the current game, *Wisp's Lost Lanterns* (Halloween edition, October 30 – November 1, 2026). Packages and prices on this page describe that experience; future titles may have different formats and prices.

## The story you're staging

Wisp — a small, wandering will-o'-the-wisp, and Dr. Z's escaped first creation — has lost her six glow lanterns, the lights that show her the way home. Without them she flickers like a dying candle. Teams become Lantern Keepers: follow the trail across six stops, solve the puzzle at each, and relight one lantern at a time. Every lantern relit makes Wisp brighter and braver — and when all six burn, the quest ends in the Lantern Parade, where Wisp lights your way home too. As host, you are not running a generic scavenger hunt: you are setting the trail Wisp's Lantern Keepers walk, from the Welcome Pumpkin at the front door to the Lantern Parade at the end. Your signs, your route, and your finale photo are the night Wisp gets her light back.

## What hosting involves

1. **Pick a tier** that fits your group — [Choosing a Tier](tiers.md).
2. **Coordinate with the Lab** before you announce anything (see below).
3. **Set up** — about an hour of printing and placing signs, spread over the two weeks before your event: [Before the Event](before-the-event.md).
4. **Run it** — gather your players, read a 30-second briefing, and roam while the game runs itself: [Running the Event](running-the-event.md).
5. **Wrap up** — finale, group photo, and feedback: [After the Event](after-the-event.md).

## What's in the kit

| Item | Format | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 6 stop signs | Printable PDF, Letter or A4 | Print, trim, mount |
| Start poster | Printable PDF | Post at the gathering point |
| Access codes | Email + printable card | Hand to teams (or post one code) |
| Organizer guide | 1 page | Read once, keep handy — placement map, answer key, tiebreakers, scoring |
| Finisher badges | Printable PDF | Cut out, hand out at the finale |
| Map preview | Web link | Check your route before the day |
| Remote support | Email | Limited, best-effort email support; replies may be delayed on Halloween |

Each stop also carries its own location story and puzzle, a 3-hint ladder, and a printed sign code as a camera-free fallback. The player pages are offline-safe once loaded — no app for anyone to install.

## Use the generic kit in your space

Every flat kit uses the same generic quest text. It is not written around **your** space, and there is no setup call to customize stories or puzzles. Pick waypoints that fit the kit's six stops — a backyard works; stops can be 20 feet apart. The puzzles carry the game, not the hiking.

## Before you announce your event

Availability depends on the current game, event window, venue, setup, and support capacity. Before announcing an event, coordinate with the Lab on:

- The exact kit and materials included
- The price and any fundraising terms
- The event window and organizer responsibilities
- Accessibility, safety, route, and weather arrangements
- What support is available and how to reach it
- Whether the Lab can support your location
