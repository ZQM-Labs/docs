# Running the Event

Day-of, you are the host — not the game master. The game runs itself from the first QR scan; your job is the route check, the briefing, and the finale.

## 1 hour before

- [ ] Re-walk the route. Signs wander; wind is the usual suspect.
- [ ] Open the [Day-Of Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) page on your phone and keep it handy.
- [ ] Charge check: your phone and the backup battery.

## The start

Gather players at the start poster, hand out (or post) the access code, and read the 30-second briefing:

> "Six stops, scan the QR at each, solve the puzzle, hints cost you nothing but pride. Most solved wins."

Then start the clock.

## During the game

- **Roam, don't hover.** Wander the route, enjoy being the person who knows where everything is, and let teams play.
- Teams share one phone each and move at their own pace; a full run takes 30–60 minutes.
- If a team falls behind, remind them hints are free — and if a team is truly stuck, walk them to the next stop yourself. Fun beats fairness.
- The Lab monitors [zqmcomputing@gmail.com](mailto:zqmcomputing@gmail.com) during every event window — if something breaks that the [troubleshooting page](troubleshooting.md) doesn't cover, email.

## The finale

- Gather everyone at the last stop.
- Tally solved counts. Tied? Your answer key includes a **tiebreaker question** — read it aloud, first correct shout wins. Still tied? Declare co-champions; it's a party.
- Hand out the finisher badges.
- **Take the group photo** — see [After the Event](after-the-event.md) for what happens to it.
