# How to Play

A ZQM Labs quest is a walking scavenger hunt with a story. Everything below is the current game, *Wisp's Lost Lanterns*; other titles may differ in theme and mechanics.

## The shape of the game

- **6 stops.** Each stop has a printed QR sign, a short location story, and a puzzle.
- **Teams of 3–5 work best.** One phone per team is all you need — 6 to 60 players can play the same quest.
- **30–60 minutes** for all six stops, depending on walking distance and how long your team argues about riddles. (You will.)
- **Most solved wins.** Your host's answer key carries a tiebreaker if two teams finish level.

## What you need

- A phone with a browser and a camera. **No app to install** — the player page runs in the browser and works offline once loaded.
- Walking shoes. That's the whole packing list.

## Playing a stop

1. Walk to the stop and **scan the QR sign**. If the camera struggles, every sign also prints a **sign code** (like `SAMPLE-3`) you can type into the player page manually.
2. The player checks you're in the right place (within about 30 m). If GPS drifts — tree cover and buildings do that — the manual sign code keeps the game moving.
3. Read the story, solve the puzzle, enter your answer.
4. Stuck? Use the **hint ladder**: 3 hints at every stop, and the third is the "Ranger assist." Hints are free — they cost you nothing but pride.

## The Dial

Every quest runs at one of four Dial levels. Your host picks the level; the same stops play differently at each:

| Dial | Who it's for |
|---|---|
| **Cozy** | All ages — the family setting |
| **Spooky** | Teens and up |
| **After Dark** | Adults |
| **Overdrive** | Competitive groups — the office setting |
